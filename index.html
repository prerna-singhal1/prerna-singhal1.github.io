About Me

I'm a student at Empros International School — serving as Head Girl and President of the CS Club. I build AI systems that actually ship to real users: multilingual voice companions for elderly care, end-to-end healthcare networks, and agentic tools that automate complex workflows.

My technical obsession spans agentic AI architectures, memory systems, model fine-tuning, and system design. Recognized by AIIMS Delhi — India's #1 medical institute — for healthcare AI impact, and shortlisted among India's top 50 students by ISRO for the prestigious YUVIKA program.

I write research papers, publish books, win international olympiads, and run a non-profit — often simultaneously. The best builders aren't just technically strong; they deeply understand the humans their systems serve.

Beyond Code

Viksit Youth — Non-Profit

I founded Viksit Youth — a student-driven non-profit focused on environmental awareness, civic education, and elder care. VIKSIT stands for the younger generation advancing toward responsible and aware citizenship.

We've organized large-scale cleanup drives, provided sustained support to senior citizens in old age homes, and delivered foundational civic and science sessions at government schools across the region.

Every initiative is student-led, with short-term measurable goals for environmental good and community uplift. The best technology is useless if we don't also show up for our communities in person.
